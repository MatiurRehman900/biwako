About Us

Founded in 2003, our company began its journey in the used car export business. In the early years, we primarily exported vehicles to Russia, Pakistan, the UK, UAE, and other international markets. As our network and expertise expanded, we identified a growing global demand for high-quality used auto parts.

Over time, we gradually diversified from complete car exports into the used automotive parts industry. Today, we specialize in the bulk export of used auto parts and proudly operate as a reliable supplier to markets such as the Philippines, Pakistan, UAE, Russia, Africa, and Latin American countries.

With over two decades of industry experience, we have built strong partnerships, gained extensive market knowledge, and established ourselves as a trusted name in the global auto parts export business.

We are a multilingual team, capable of communicating in: English, Japanese, Russian, Urdu, and Pashto, enabling us to serve a diverse international customer base effectively.

Our commitment to quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction continues to drive our growth as we deliver value and trusted automotive solutions around the world.

Why Choose Us

1. Strategic Location

Our yard is located in Toyama Prefecture, close to three major ports. We are:

  • Only 5 minutes from Toyama Shinko Port
  • 20 minutes from Toyama Iwase Port
  • 25 minutes from Toyama Fushiki Port

This prime location makes us one of the most convenient and efficient hubs for exports to Russia and other international destinations. Our proximity to multiple ports ensures faster loading, reduced transportation costs, and timely shipments.

2. Strong and Smooth Supply Chain

Thanks to our long-term relationships with local car dealers and our memberships in major Japanese auto auctions, we maintain a strong and reliable supply chain. We can source all types of vehicles, including:

  • Passenger cars
  • Vans
  • Trucks
  • Heavy machinery

This ensures a consistent and wide range of inventory for our customers worldwide.

3. Multilingual Staff

Our team consists of multilingual professionals who can communicate seamlessly with customers from different regions. We speak: English, Japanese, Russian, Urdu, and Pashto.

This helps us provide better support, clear communication, and smooth coordination throughout the buying and shipping process.

4. Licensed and Authorized

We are a fully licensed and registered exporter in Japan. Our official licenses and certifications allow us to:

  • Participate in all major Japanese auto auctions
  • Legally dismantle and recycle Japanese vehicles
  • Handle chlorofluorocarbon (CFC) removal, airbag dismantling, vehicle scrapping, and parts dismantling in compliance with Japanese regulations

These certifications ensure that our operations are safe, environmentally responsible, and fully aligned with Japan's strict automotive recycling standards.
